## Owner-approved requested-period rule (2026-11-10)

Confirmed first availability after the requested first trading session returns SHORT_HISTORY and blocks every portfolio and SPY series. It never moves the shared start. Choosing a shorter period creates a fresh investment; require two completed shared sessions. Missing bars/provider failures do not establish first availability. Requested-window coverage can be asserted separately with periodCoverageVerified while inceptionVerified remains false; its firstAvailable field is then only the covered boundary and must be on/before the requested start. This flag is an upstream coverage attestation, not permission to ignore missing required closes/actions or proof of lifetime inception. Personal provisional limitations still apply.

Source requests and action entitlement validation honor the selected period. Events with an established ex/effective date outside it do not affect a fresh investment; an event without an interpretable date cannot be safely excluded. Gaps inside the period and unsupported actions inside it still fail. Full cached windows can serve narrower periods, and partial windows cannot satisfy wider ones. Decimal arithmetic, drifting weights, splits, ex-date receivables/payment-date reinvestment, SPY and date clamping are unchanged. Earlier genuine-short-history shortening requirements below are superseded.

Inputs are asserted by the caller to be verified. The calendar is an ordered, unique list of New York exchange session dates with an inclusive coverage interval and `complete: true`; its dates identify completed sessions, including holidays, half days and DST as resolved upstream. It must cover the requested window and any payment-date roll-forward used by the result. Absence of a date means non-session only within that verified interval. A list alone cannot prove completeness. Asset inputs assert raw, split-unadjusted, dividend-unadjusted closes, genuine first available date or an explicit covered-window boundary, complete requested-period prices and corporate actions, and coverage across the requested interval. Failures and unknown attestations stop execution. Do not infer a provider's bar completion, actions or pagination from this contract. All sessions from common start through end need a positive close for every asset. A verified later first available date blocks the requested shared period; a missing close after that date is an error. Start-date distributions confer no entitlement; splits on the entry date precede purchase. For later dates, apply split to shares first, then accrue ex-date dividends per post-split share, then settle payable receivables at the close. An action whose interpretation differs must be rejected upstream. Payment on a non-session rolls forward, and outstanding entitlements retain face value. Actions on the same date of the same type are additive for dividends and multiplicative for splits. Unsupported events fail.

Technical conventions: decimal.js 10.6.0 runs in a private 40-significant-digit context, rounding each arithmetic operation to nearest with ties to even; division is finite and not exact. Inputs are plain unsigned decimal strings, at most 18 fractional and 18 integer digits. Prices, amounts, weights, split ratios and dividend amounts are positive. At most 50 unique assets, 3 portfolios, 5000 sessions and 10000 actions total; decimal result exponents outside -900 to 900 fail with NUMERIC_LIMIT. Display independently rounds to two decimal places, ties to even. Fixed rational fixture oracles are compared with absolute error at most 1e-30 for these small fixtures. This tolerance is an experimental fixture bound, not a universal error bound across all permitted workloads: adversarial repeated operations may accumulate error; further numerical analysis is required before integration. Returns and drawdowns derive from the same unrounded daily series; displayed two-decimal chart points are presentation only.

Presets use a verified latest completed session, calendar subtraction with target-month clamping, then roll forward via the verified session list; YTD starts January 1 and Max at the calendar coverage start. Genuine later first availability blocks rather than moving the common start. Requested and actual periods are returned. A calendar that cannot prove the relevant boundaries fails closed. A preset always runs a fresh investment.

Error codes distinguish invalid inputs, unverified data, insufficient coverage, missing prices, unsupported actions and numeric limits. ## Approved scope and remaining evidence

The comparison benchmark is the SPY ETF, not the index. The simulation applies no additional taxes, brokerage fees, spreads or slippage, and does not deduct fund operating expenses a second time. Provider-reported dividend cash retains any source withholding under the owner-approved Alpaca interpretation above. Historical results are hypothetical; past performance does not guarantee future returns. Selection with hindsight does not establish that assets could have been selected in advance. Interface results must show requested and actual dates, blocking limitation explanations and assumptions; this offline module does not implement interface copy. Unsupported spin-offs and other non-split/non-dividend events stop the calculation.

A provider adapter must independently establish calendar and bar finality, session timestamp mapping, revision delays, raw price basis, first availability, complete pagination and access, action correction coverage, payment dates and normalized units. Neither the calendar supplied by a caller nor the decimal engine proves these claims. Alpaca adjustment modes have not been shown to reproduce payment-close reinvestment; an adjusted dividend series combined with explicit dividends would double count. Provider investigation references: [historical bars](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/stockbars), [corporate actions](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1), [deprecated legacy announcements](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/get-v2-corporate_actions-announcements-1), and [staff correction discussion](https://forum.alpaca.markets/t/bad-data-incorrect-price-adjustment-for-cash-dividend/18614). These are research references, not validation or permission for public use. No provider calls or account validation occurred here.

A measured cache/refresh budget and a separately scoped server integration remain future tasks; no storage architecture or provider is approved by this engine. Action identifiers must be stable and unique per asset; duplicate identities fail. The output records caller-supplied data provenance and a method revision, without validating that provenance. For the repeat-reinvestment fixture, the relative comparison tolerance is 1e-32 against the independent exact expression 3 * (4/3)^299. The small-policy fixtures use 1e-30 absolute tolerance. These measured cases do not establish a universal error bound for all allowed input sequences; numerical error analysis at resource limits remains a review/integration gate.
