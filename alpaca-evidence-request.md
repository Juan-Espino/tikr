# Alpaca evidence request draft

Prepared 2026-11-05. **Draft only; not sent.** Tikr is a personal, non-commercial historical portfolio simulation. We use raw SIP daily bars for stocks and ETFs, with dividends and splits handled separately. We need an evidence-backed as-of methodology, not a promise that historical data can never change.

## Questions for Alpaca market-data support

1. For the historical stock bars API with `feed=sip` and `adjustment=raw`, what exact daily-close construction applies across CTA and UTP tapes? Please identify the session boundaries, eligible trade conditions, timestamp/order and tie-breaking rules, precision/rounding, treatment of late, corrected and canceled trades, and any differences from the published aggregation FAQ. Is there an independently accessible reference or reproducible SIP-trade procedure that produces the same close?
2. When and how can a historical daily bar change after its session? Is there a documented completeness or correction cutoff, revision identifier, change feed, or query method to discover changes to older bars without refetching all history? How do normal sessions, early closes and daylight-saving changes affect this? Is next-day 09:00 New York time supported as anything beyond an operational buffer?
3. The corporate-actions endpoint filters by `process_date` and documents possible delays. How can a client establish complete action coverage for an arbitrary historical ex-date/effective-date interval, including actions processed before or after that interval? What lookback, lookahead, retention and data-quality rules apply, and can incomplete records later disappear or change?
4. How are late corrections, cancellations and deletions discovered and linked to prior records? Does the corporate-action event stream provide complete historical mutation replay from a documented earliest point, and what are its retention, entitlement, ordering, reconnect and gap-recovery guarantees? Can an endpoint snapshot and stream establish a complete baseline and ongoing changes? Please describe limitations and unsupported event types.
5. For ordinary cash dividends and forward/reverse splits, what are the exact meanings and share bases of `rate`, `old_rate` and `new_rate`? When a split and dividend share an effective/ex-date, how should entitlement and dividend units be ordered and normalized? Please provide an example including dates and units. How are special distributions, due bills, symbol/identifier changes and other unsupported events identifiable?
6. Are there documented coverage limitations, provider delays, entitlement restrictions or correction policies that prevent asserting complete action and price coverage for a specific asset and interval? Please link the relevant documentation and distinguish operational guidance from guarantees.

## Independent close-reference lead

[Massive's trade-eligibility documentation](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/how-does-massive-create-the-open-high-low-close-volume-aggregate-bars) describes consolidated daily-bar update rules and SIP condition mappings; its published unadjusted NVIDIA examples already matched three captured closes. It is a **candidate**, not a verified matching reference: compare every relevant CTA/UTP condition, interval, ordering, correction and precision rule against Alpaca, then verify historical entitlement, permitted independent validation use, cost and access. No account, data request, purchase or rights conclusion is implied. A suitable licensed SIP trade reconstruction is an alternative if its rules and corrections can be independently established.
